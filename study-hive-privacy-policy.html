🐝 Study Hive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Study Hive is a study countdown, focus and motivation tool that runs entirely in your web browser. This policy explains, in plain language, how your information is handled.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in Study Hive — your countdown goal, subjects, tasks, notes, flashcards, mood, streaks and settings — is saved only in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. It is not uploaded to any server and there is no account or login.

Because data lives in the browser, clearing your browser data / site storage, or using a different device, will start you fresh. Use the in-app Backup Center (Settings) to export a copy before clearing data.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Features that reach the internet (optional)

  • Background music plays from a file you provide; the built-in lofi tones are generated locally.
  • Weather and Bee AI helper features may request public web services if enabled; these are optional and you can use the app fully without them.
